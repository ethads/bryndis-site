Features

Quiet tools
for loud moments.

Bryndis is shaped around one question: what would you actually use, in the moment you actually needed it? Four free core features, two paid modules for specific situations, all built on the same two-second, one-thumb principle.

Core · Free 01 · Hold-to-alert SOS

One gesture.
The whole circle.

Open Bryndis, press and hold the home tile for two seconds, and an alert lands with every trusted contact at the same time — push first, SMS fallback, complete with live GPS coordinates and a one-tap "I'm here" reply.

  • Two-second hold. No accidental triggers. Cancel by lifting your thumb.
  • Live location. Coordinates with accuracy radius, refreshed every 30 seconds until the alert is cleared.
  • SMS fallback. If push isn't acknowledged in five seconds, Twilio sends a clear SMS with the map link.
  • Always call 000 first in a life-threatening emergency. Bryndis is a companion to emergency services, not a replacement.
Core · Free 02 · Trusted contacts

Up to five people.
No app required for them.

Bryndis isn't a social network. There are no friend requests, no profiles, no public anything. You add up to five trusted contacts by phone number — partners, parents, housemates, the friend who answers at 2am — and they're notified by SMS, no install needed.

  • SMS-first. Contacts don't need to download anything. The link in their SMS opens a live map in any browser.
  • One-tap callback. Every alert SMS includes a tap-to-call number, so the first action is always a voice connection.
  • Priority order. Set a primary contact who is called automatically when SOS is triggered.
  • Consent built in. Adding someone sends them a one-time SMS that explains what they've been added to, with an easy opt-out.
Core · Free 03 · Check-in timer

If you don't check in,
they will.

Set a window before doing something where you'd want someone to notice if you didn't come back. Fifteen minutes, an hour, the length of a date. Bryndis nudges you for a check-in. If you don't respond, your trusted contacts are alerted — automatically.

  • Flexible windows. Quick presets for 15min, 30min, 1hr, 2hr — or pick a custom time.
  • Friendly nudges. A check-in reminder at the right time, with a one-tap "I'm safe" button.
  • Auto-escalate on missed check-in. Two-minute grace, then full alert — same chain as SOS.
  • Cancel any time. One tap, no questions, no log left behind.
Core · Free 04 · Find your car

Drop a pin.
Walk back, no signal needed.

Park, tap once. Bryndis remembers exactly where you left the car — and an AR compass overlay points you back through your camera view, with a glowing arrow that works even in a basement car park with no GPS.

  • One-tap park. Captures GPS, magnetic heading, and a quick photo for visual confirmation.
  • AR compass. Camera overlay with a soft glowing arrow and distance counter. Works without an internet connection.
  • Designed for car parks. The feature most often used at night, alone, in unfamiliar buildings — built to perform there first.
  • Auto-clears. The pin clears when you start driving again. Nothing to remember to delete.
Optional · Paid module Date Buddy

For first dates,
and second ones too.

A safety module designed for the moments around dating: meeting someone new, sharing your evening plans with one chosen friend, and a soft auto-check-in that knows the difference between "I'm having a great time" and "something's off."

  • Date plan share. Send one nominated friend the venue, time, and a photo of who you're meeting — encrypted, expires automatically.
  • Mid-date check-in. A gentle prompt at the halfway mark — three taps for "all good," one tap for "get me out," nothing for "escalate quietly."
  • Fake call. Trigger a believable incoming call from "Mum" with a tap, to give yourself a clean exit.
Optional · Paid module Rideshare Tracker

The trip,
witnessed.

For Uber, Ola, Didi, taxis. Capture the driver, the plate, the route — then have Bryndis quietly watch the journey. Off-route, prolonged stops, or arrival far from the destination triggers a check-in. Smooth ride? Nothing happens.

  • Driver capture. One screen tap stores driver name, plate number and rideshare service, encrypted and shared only on escalation.
  • Live route monitoring. Bryndis watches the route on-device against the destination. Deviations over 500m trigger a soft check-in.
  • "I'm home" cancellation. One tap closes the trip when you're safely inside. Otherwise it auto-escalates after the expected ETA + 10 minutes.
