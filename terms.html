2. What Bryndis is, and isn't

Bryndis is a mobile application that allows you to:

  • Trigger an alert ("SOS") to a list of trusted contacts you have chosen.
  • Set a check-in timer that escalates to your trusted contacts if you fail to confirm you are safe.
  • Drop a location pin and navigate back using an augmented-reality compass.
  • Optionally subscribe to additional modules (currently Date Buddy and Rideshare Tracker).

Bryndis is not:

  • An emergency dispatch service.

  • A medical, mental health, or domestic violence support service.
  • A substitute for calling 000 (or your local emergency number).
