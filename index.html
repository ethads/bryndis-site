Three principles

Designed by people
who actually walk home.

A safety app is only useful if it works when you can't think clearly. Bryndis was built around three non-negotiable principles — the things every other detail bends to.

01

Two-second activation

From locked screen to your trusted circle being alerted in under two seconds. No menus, no logins, no friction at the moment it matters.

02

One-thumb navigation

Every critical action reachable with the thumb of the hand that's holding the phone. Because the other hand might be holding keys, a leash, or a door.

03

No false alarms

A short undo window on every alert and a discreet visual language — no flashing red, no panic colours. A safety net you can carry every day without crying wolf.

What it does

Quiet tools
for loud moments.

Four core features, designed to fade into the background until you need them. Free, forever. No ads, no upsells in the middle of a SOS.

One-tap SOS

Tap the SOS bar on the home screen. You get a moment to undo, then your location, a short context note, and an alert are sent to every trusted contact at once — push notification first, SMS fallback, the moment it might matter.

Free · Core

Trusted contacts

Up to five people who can be reached instantly. They don't need the app installed. They get a clear SMS with your live location, a one-tap call back, and a link to track your trip if you've started one.

Free · Core

Check-in timer

Heading somewhere? Set a window — 15 minutes, an hour, the length of a date. If you don't check in by then, your trusted contacts are alerted automatically. Cancel any time with a single tap.

Free · Core

Find your car

Drop a pin when you park. Bryndis remembers exactly where you left it — and an AR compass overlays a glowing arrow on your camera view to walk you back, even in a multi-storey car park with no signal.

Free · Core

Bryndis exists because no one should walk home wondering if anyone would notice. The same logic carries further than an app can. Privacy promise

Your location
belongs to you.

Personal safety apps that quietly sell location data are not safety apps. Bryndis is built on a different premise: the people you choose, the data you control, hosted in Australia.

  • Australian-hosted, Australian-governed Backed by Supabase Sydney. Your data does not leave the country without your action.
  • No third-party advertisers, ever Bryndis will never sell, rent, or share your location, contacts, or check-ins with advertisers. Stated as a binding term in our Privacy Policy.
  • Delete your account in two taps Account deletion is one-tap from settings. All personal data is permanently erased within 30 days, with confirmation.
  • Privacy Act 1988 (APP) compliant Full alignment with the thirteen Australian Privacy Principles, including breach notification, access, and correction rights.
